No accounts · No servers · No ads · No tracking · No third-party SDKs

1. Information we collect

None. The app does not collect, transmit, or store any data, personal or otherwise. There is no sign-up, no operating server, and no analytics or advertising SDK.

2. What stays on your device

The minimal settings needed for play are stored only on your iPhone:

  • Your display name and character choice
  • This gathering's penalty tally (shown in-app only)

This never leaves your device and is deleted when you delete the app.

3. Why we ask for Local Network permission

The game runs by connecting phones in the same room directly (peer-to-peer), without internet. Local Network (and Wi-Fi/Bluetooth) access is used solely for this connection. The only data exchanged is game signals (choices and results), travelling between participants' phones and never to any external server.

4. Sharing with third parties

Since nothing is collected, there is nothing to share. No third-party SDKs, ad networks, or analytics tools are used.

5. Children's privacy

The app collects no personal information from any user, including children.
